Scope & responsibilities

Clear division of work

Typical roles — your sealed drawings and final quote control the contract. This is educational guidance, not a guarantee of every line item on every job.

Typically included / coordinated

Stamps Steel PEB erection scope

  • ✓Structural erection of primary frames when a full erection package is quoted
  • ✓Secondary members — purlins, girts, and bracing per the erection drawings
  • ✓Roof & wall panels and related trim as specified in the package
  • ✓Framed openings for overhead doors, walk doors, and windows as detailed
  • ✓Basic weather-tight installation of the PEB shell (panels, trim, sealants per system)
  • ✓Coordination & support — delivery timing, drawing questions, and specialty-system notes (IMP, standing seam, liners) when included

Owner / site responsibilities

Typically by owner or GC

  • ○Foundation design and construction to PEB reactions and local code
  • ○Site prep — graded pad, drainage, and crane/equipment access
  • ○Access roads and material laydown space suitable for delivery trucks and lifts
  • ○Utilities — power, water, and temporary services as needed for the site
  • ○Permits and approvals with the local AHJ
  • ○Final inspections and any owner-required third-party reviews
  • ○Interiors — insulation beyond the PEB shell package (if separate), offices, MEP, floors, and finishes

Why professional PEB erection matters

Sequence, alignment, and the system warranty

A PEB is an engineered system. Field sequence and fit-up affect plumbness, weather sealing, and how the building performs under wind and snow — and how manufacturer requirements are satisfied in the field.

Design your building in 3D first →

Erection sequence

Frames, bracing, secondaries, then panels — out of order work creates fit-up problems and rework.

Bracing & alignment

Temporary and permanent bracing keep the structure plumb until the diaphragm is complete.

Weather sealing

Panel laps, trim, and sealants must follow the system details for a durable envelope.

Safety & warranty

Proper lifts, fall protection, and installation practices protect people and the product system.
