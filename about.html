Frame, roof, walls, extras

At One World Steel, we bring more than a decade of hands-on experience in metal buildings (PEBs). We quote complete kits — steel frame, roof and walls, insulation and doors — and we can install on your slab or piers. Our work covers planning, quoting, delivery, and installation with quality, safety, and craftsmanship first.

We work on shops, storage, farms, hangars, churches, barndos, warehouses, and commercial buildings — from a single garage to a multi-building site.

Roof, walls, and extras

Insulated metal panels, standing-seam and screw-down roofs, liners, canopies, and trim — finished so they weather well and look right.

Specialty steel

Roof curbs, framed openings, mezzanines, stairs, crane support, and related steel — coordinated with the manufacturer, engineer, and builder.

How we work
