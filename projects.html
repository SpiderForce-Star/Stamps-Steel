Every project starts with the right frame. Clear-span or modular, single slope or lean-to — primary steel, purlins, girts, and framed openings define how your building works.

Full framing guide

Frame types

Straight & tapered clear span, 1–3 row modular, single slope, and lean-tos — matched to width, clear height, and use.

Purlins & wall girts

Secondary members that carry roof and wall panels into the primary frames — Z-purlins, girts, eave struts.

Framed openings

Overhead doors, walk doors, windows, louvers, and roof vents — headers and jambs integrated with the wall system.
