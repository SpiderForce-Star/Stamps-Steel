Why steel for churches

A sanctuary that actually spans

Pre-engineered metal buildings are built around wide, open frames — not a skinny chapel kit. Stamps Steel packages 50-foot-plus clear spans so the nave, fellowship hall, or gym can stay column-free for seating, acoustics, and future reconfiguration.

The steel shell is the economical part. Value engineering then layers the architecture congregations care about: covered entries, glass storefronts, steeples, classroom wings, insulation, and interior finishes. You get a real PEB — long rectangular volume, standing-seam roof, vertical wall panels — dressed for worship.

  • · Sanctuaries and auditoriums with 50′–150′+ clear spans
  • · Fellowship halls, gyms, and multi-purpose rooms
  • · Attached education / office wings on the same package
  • · High-R insulation or IMPs for conditioned worship space

What we package

Primary frame
