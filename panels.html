Profiles

Panel Types

Typical commercial PEB roof and wall profiles. Specs below are industry-standard ranges used for selection — final gauge, coverage, and slope limits depend on the manufacturer package on your order.

PBR / R-Loc Style

Most popular

Purlin-bearing rib (PBR) exposed-fastener panel — the workhorse for commercial and agricultural PEBs. Used for roofs and walls; installable vertically or horizontally.

  • · ~36" coverage; tall ribs with purlin-bearing leg
  • · Common gauges: 26 painted; 26/24 Galvalume®
  • · Roof & wall; typical min. roof slope ~½:12
  • · Strong panel-to-panel overlap for commercial loads

Industry references: Central States R-Loc®, MBCI PBR

R-Panel

Classic exposed-fastener profile with a clean ribbed look for agricultural, industrial, and commercial roofs and walls. Economical and widely available.

  • · Similar appearance family to PBR (overlap detail varies)
  • · Excellent value for shops, barns, and warehouses
  • · Painted and Galvalume® finishes
  • · Roof and wall applications

Industry references: MBCI R-Panel / related exposed-fastener lines

Multi-Rib / Panel-Loc Style

Popular low- to mid-rib exposed-fastener panels for versatile roof and wall applications — strong aesthetics at a competitive price point.

  • · Versatile coverage for residential-scale and light commercial
  • · Often specified with SMP paint systems
  • · Optional striations for flat-area stiffness
  • · Roof, wall, and accent applications

Industry references: Central States Panel-Loc® / Panel-Loc Plus®, M-Loc®

Standing Seam — Snap-Lock

Architectural roof system with concealed fasteners and raised vertical seams. Clean lines for commercial and higher-end shop exteriors.

  • · Concealed clips / snap engagement (system-specific)
  • · Improved weather performance vs. exposed fastener
  • · Common 24-ga painted architectural finishes
  • · Slope requirements vary by system

Industry references: Central States Central Snap® / similar; MBCI snap systems

Standing Seam — Mechanically Seamed

Field-seamed systems for superior weather-tight performance on lower slopes and high-performance commercial roofs.

  • · Single- or double-lock seaming (system-specific)
  • · Preferred for low-slope commercial applications
  • · Structural clip attachment for thermal movement
  • · Long service life with proper detailing

Industry references: Central Seam Plus® / Central-Loc® families; MBCI Double-Lok® & related

7.2 & Deep-Rib Wall Panels

Deep-corrugation wall and liner panels for industrial aesthetics, strength, and economical coverage on large wall planes.

  • · Distinctive industrial profile
  • · Strong spanning characteristics
  • · Wall, partition, and select roof uses by product
  • · Painted and metallic finishes

Industry references: Central States 7.2 Panel; comparable MBCI deep-rib walls

Board & Batten

Vertical accent siding for modern shops, offices, and storefronts — pairs well with PBR field panels and standing-seam roofs.

  • · Architectural wall aesthetic
  • · Great for office ends and entry façades
  • · Coordinate colors with roof and trim
  • · Metal durability with residential-style look

Industry references: Central States Board & Batten; similar MBCI wall systems

Acrylic Galvalume® / Bare Metallic

Unpainted Metallic Finish

Galvalume® substrate without color coat — bright metallic look, excellent corrosion resistance, and a classic commercial PEB appearance.

  • · Often 24- or 26-gauge acrylic-coated Galvalume®
  • · Popular on agricultural and industrial roofs
  • · No chalk/fade color warranty (substrate warranties apply)
  • · Pairs with painted walls or full metallic envelope

Available on many Central States and MBCI structural panels

Finishes

Standard Color Palette

Common commercial metal building finish colors used across major panel manufacturers. Swatches are approximate for screen viewing — always approve a physical sample or manufacturer color chart before final selection. Availability varies by gauge, paint system (e.g. SMP / PVDF), and mill.

Paint systems

  • SMP — Siliconized polyester; strong everyday commercial performance and color selection.
  • PVDF (Kynar-type) — Premium architectural chalk/fade resistance for high-visibility projects.
  • Galvalume® — Unpainted metallic substrate with excellent corrosion resistance.

How we help you choose

  • Match roof, wall, and trim for a cohesive exterior.
  • Consider Middle Tennessee sun exposure and heat on dark roofs.
  • Coordinate with doors, canopies, and accessory finishes.
  • Confirm current manufacturer color charts on your quote.

Quick Selection Guide

Shops & Garages

PBR or multi-rib roof + wall is the most common package. Light Stone or Polar White walls with a contrasting roof (Charcoal, Burnished Slate, or Galvalume®) keep the look sharp and practical.

Agricultural

PBR / R-Panel with Galvalume® or Forest/Hunter green remains a regional favorite. Focus on durability, ventilation, and clear-span utility over architectural detail.

Commercial / Auto

PBR field panels with optional standing-seam roof or board-and-batten office accents. Medium Bronze, Slate Gray, and Black read professional for street-facing façades.
