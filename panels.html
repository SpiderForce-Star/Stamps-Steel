Quick Selection Guide

Shops & Garages

PBR / R-Loc roof + wall is the most common package. Light Stone or Polar White walls with Charcoal, Burnished Slate, or Galvalume® roofs stay practical and sharp.

Agricultural

R-Loc / PBR or multi-rib with Galvalume® or Hunter/Forest green. Prioritize durability, ventilation, and clear-span utility.

Commercial / Auto

PBR field panels with optional standing seam (Double-Lok / Ultra-Dek / LokSeam) or accent walls. Medium Bronze, slate grays, and black read professional.
