PEB building systems

How a Stamps Steel package is built

A complete pre-engineered metal building is more than a shell. Primary and secondary framing, roof and wall panels, insulation or IMPs, openings, and accessories are specified together for your site loads and use group.

Structure

Primary & secondary framing

Rigid frames, purlins, girts, and framed openings — the skeleton that carries wind, snow, and clear-span requirements.

View framing →

Envelope

Roof & wall panels

PBR, multi-rib, standing seam, and specialty profiles with color systems for PEB walls and roofs.

Explore panels →

Thermal

Fiberglass insulation systems

Single-layer PSK-VR (3″ R-10 · 4″ R-13 · 6″ R-19) and High-R multi-layer cavity systems for controlled interiors.

View insulation →

High-R envelope

Insulated metal panels (IMP)

Factory-foamed wall and standing-seam roof panels · R-16 to R-50 · 26/24 ga skins for higher thermal performance.

View IMPs →

Openings & add-ons

Accessories

Doors, operators, ventilation, canopies, windows, roof curbs, and hardware coordinated with the PEB package.
